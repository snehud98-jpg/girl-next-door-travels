Little things that help

Travel tips from one friend to another

Simple ideas for a smoother, more affordable trip.

🧳

Pack lighter

Check baggage rules before booking and plan outfits you can mix and match.

🏡

Choose stays wisely

Compare the full cost, including cleaning fees, city taxes and transport to attractions.

📸

Capture the memories

Plan a few special photo stops, but leave time to enjoy the destination without a camera.
